# Usability protocol

How to find out whether representative users can complete the primary workflow reliably and
prefer it to their current process. The automated checks (accessibility script, scale test,
workflow test) say the application cannot get in their way; only people can say it is better.

## Who

Five people who maintain a fictional universe today: a novelist or series author, a tabletop game
master, a worldbuilder who keeps a wiki or notes, a screenwriter or showrunner, an editor or
continuity reader. At least two have more than 100 notes in their current tool.

## Session (60 minutes, recorded with consent)

1. **Context, 5 min.** What they use now, what hurts, what they would never give up.
2. **Setup, 5 min.** Fresh install. No explanation of the application.
3. **Tasks, 35 min,** read aloud one at a time, no help unless stuck for 2 minutes:
   1. Create a project for a universe of your own and describe it in one line.
   2. Add five things that exist in it, of at least three different kinds.
   3. Say that one of them is the parent of another.
   4. Add a fact about a character that is only true for a period.
   5. Record an event with a date and who took part.
   6. Add a chapter and say who appears in it.
   7. Find the page of the character you made first, from anywhere, without the mouse.
   8. Find what contradicts what in what you wrote.
   9. You deleted something by mistake: bring it back.
   10. Take a copy of everything out, as a document.
4. **Compare, 10 min.** Import nothing; take one real piece of their current material and enter
   it. Ask what they would do next in each tool.
5. **Close, 5 min.** What they would miss, what annoyed them, whether they would use it.

## What to record

| Measure | How | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Task completion | Done without help, with help, not done | 9 of 10 tasks done unaided by 4 of 5 people |
| Time on task | Seconds from reading to done | Median under 2 minutes for tasks 2 to 7 |
| Errors | Wrong turns, undo uses, error messages seen | No task with a blocking error |
| Findability | Where they looked first for each thing | First guess right for 8 of 10 tasks |
| Preference | "Which would you rather keep your universe in?" and why | At least 3 of 5 prefer Ontara for something meaningful |
| Trust | "Would you put a year of work in it?" | At least 4 of 5 yes, with reasons |

## What to do with it

Each observation becomes one of: a defect (fix), a missing text or default (fix), a missing
capability (decision), or a difference of taste (record, do not act). Run the protocol again
after changes; the targets above are the exit condition of the phase.
